## With your own child or student

You don't need to know the subject well to help. Each lesson is built so you can learn alongside them:

1. Open the lesson together and read **Start here** out loud. Let them guess before anyone explains.
2. Let them drive the sim. Ask the **Try this** questions and let them answer, even when they're wrong.
3. Ask them to explain the idea back to you. If they can teach you, they've got it.
4. Leave the **Practice** answers hidden until they've really tried.

Short and often works better than long and rare: 20 minutes on three days beats an hour once a week.

## In a classroom

- **Opening:** put **Start here** on the board and take a show of hands on the answer.
- **Sims:** run them on the projector and have students call out predictions before each change. In a computer lab, pairs work better than individuals.
- **Exit ticket:** the three **Check yourself** questions.
- **Homework:** the **Practice** problems; the answers include the reasoning, so students can mark their own.

## Working offline

Every sim is a plain web page with no sign-in and no tracking. Download the repository and open any `sims/<name>/index.html` file in a browser, and it works without the internet.
