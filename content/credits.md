## Ideas and inspiration

These shaped how the site teaches, without any of their material being copied:

- Explorable explanations, the format where the reader plays with a model and the text explains what they just saw
- The *Nature of Code* approach to simulating motion with vectors (the book is CC BY-NC-SA and is only cited, not reproduced)
- Research on retrieval practice, spacing and worked examples behind the **Check yourself** and **Your turn** sections
