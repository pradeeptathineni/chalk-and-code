Every subject here is taught the way I teach in person: a question first, then an explanation on the board, and then a small program you can play with until the idea clicks. Since 2018 I have tutored, taught, mentored and run seminars in physics, math, computer science and security. This is where those lessons live now.

## How every lesson works

Each lesson follows the same path, so you always know where you are:

1. **After this lesson you can** — the goals
2. **Start here** — a puzzle or question
3. **The common wrong idea** — named up front, so it can't sneak up on you
4. **The idea** — the explanation, kept short
5. **Play with it** — a simulation with three "try this" prompts
6. **Worked example**, then **your turn** with the last steps left to you
7. **Practice** with answers you can reveal
8. **Check yourself** — three quick questions
9. **Where this goes next**

## Levels

Lessons carry a level from 0 to 4, so you can tell how far along a topic is:

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | At rest | Never met this idea |
| 1 | Nudge | First meeting: the words and the picture |
| 2 | Rolling | Can do the standard problems |
| 3 | Accelerating | Can use it on a problem you haven't seen before |
| 4 | Orbit | Can teach it to someone else |
