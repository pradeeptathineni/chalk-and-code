Our eyes see one thin slice of all the light there is. This unit opens up the rest: the long waves that carry radio and wifi, the infrared glowing from every warm thing, and the short, powerful waves of ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays. One simulation runs through the whole unit: the [light spectrum](../../../sims/light-spectrum/index.html).

**Grades:** 4–8. Lessons 1 and 2 work from grade 4. Lessons 3 and 4 suit grades 5–8, and each lesson has a stretch question for grades 7–8.

**Before you start:** nothing! This unit is a good first meeting with physics.

**At home or in class:** the phone-camera-and-remote trick in lesson 3 takes a minute and never fails to get a reaction. A black light and a white T-shirt do the same for lesson 4.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone else why wifi, a TV remote and an X-ray are all "light", and why only one of them needs a lead apron, you have this unit at level 4.
