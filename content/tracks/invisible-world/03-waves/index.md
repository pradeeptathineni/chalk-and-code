Sound, light, radio, wifi, ripples and earthquakes are all waves, so this unit is the key to most of the rest of the track. One sim runs through it: the [wave machine](../../../sims/wave-machine/index.html), a rope or slinky you can shake at any frequency and speed.

**Grades:** 4–8. Lesson 1 works from grade 4. Lessons 2 and 3 use simple multiplication and division and suit grades 5–8.

| # | Lesson | Level | Sim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [What is a wave? Energy on the move](./01-what-is-a-wave.md) | 1 | wave machine |
| 2 | [Measuring waves: amplitude, wavelength, frequency and speed](./02-measuring-waves.md) | 2 | wave machine |
| 3 | [Waves meet and bounce: echoes, adding up and cancelling out](./03-waves-meet-and-bounce.md) | 2 | wave machine |

**Hands on:** a long slinky stretched on the floor shows both kinds of wave. A skipping rope tied to a door handle shows wavelength changing as you shake faster. A tray of water and a dropped pea shows ripples and reflections off the sides.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone why a duck on a pond bobs instead of being swept away, and why shaking a rope faster makes shorter waves, you have this unit at level 4.
