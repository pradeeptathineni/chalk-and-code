We live at the bottom of an ocean of air, and it pushes on us with tonnes of force. This unit shows where that push comes from, what "suction" really is, and how pressure changes with height, heat and weather. One sim runs through it: [air pressure](../../../sims/air-pressure/index.html), a box of air particles you can squeeze, pump and heat.

**Grades:** 3–8. Lesson 1 works from grade 3 with the hands-on demos. Lessons 2 and 3 suit grades 4–8.

**Hands on:** fill a glass to the brim with water, lay a card on top, hold it on and turn the glass over, then let go of the card (over a sink!). The air pushing up holds the card and the water in. A plastic syringe with your finger over the end shows squeezing and stretching air.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone how a straw really works, and why a sealed chip bag puffs up on a mountain, you have this unit at level 4.
