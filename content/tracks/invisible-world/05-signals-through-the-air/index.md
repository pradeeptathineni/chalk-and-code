Every phone call, text, video and map on your phone travels as invisible light. This unit shows how: how a steady wave is changed to carry a message, why some ways survive noise better than others, what wifi really is, and how GPS finds you by timing signals from space. Two sims go with it: [a message on a wave](../../../sims/signal-on-a-wave/index.html) and [GPS](../../../sims/gps/index.html).

**Grades:** 6–8. Each lesson has one small calculation using speed = wavelength × frequency or distance = speed × time.

**Before you start:** [Measuring waves](../03-waves/02-measuring-waves.md) and [The electromagnetic spectrum](../04-light-we-cant-see/02-the-spectrum.md).

**Hands on:** wrap a phone in aluminum foil and try calling it: the metal blocks the signal. Then try a plastic bag: no change. Walk around the house with a wifi signal app and draw a map of where it's strong.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone how a text message gets from your phone to the router, and how your phone knows where you are without sending anything, you have this unit at level 4.
