Most pushes need a touch. Three don't: gravity, magnetism and the electric force. This unit introduces the big idea behind all three, the **field**, and lets you see one with the [field lines](../../../sims/field-lines/index.html) simulation: drag magnets and charges around and watch hundreds of tiny compass needles turn.

**Grades:** 3–8. Lessons 1 and 2 work from grade 3, especially with real magnets and a compass on the table. Lesson 3 suits grades 4–8.

| # | Lesson | Level | Sim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Forces at a distance: gravity, magnetism and static](./01-forces-at-a-distance.md) | 1 | field lines |
| 2 | [Magnets and their fields: poles, compasses and the Earth](./02-magnets.md) | 1 | field lines |
| 3 | [Static electricity: balloons, sparks and lightning](./03-static-electricity.md) | 2 | field lines |

**Hands on:** two bar magnets, a compass, a handful of paperclips, a balloon and a tap. Sprinkle iron filings on paper over a magnet to see real field lines (keep them in a sealed bag so they don't stick to the magnet).

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone else why a balloon sticks to a wall and a compass points north, using the word "field", you have this unit at level 4.
