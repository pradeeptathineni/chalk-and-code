We hear only a slice of the sounds around us. Bats shout in ultrasound, elephants rumble in infrasound, and doctors look inside the body with sound millions of times too high for any ear. One sim runs through the unit: [who can hear it?](../../../sims/hearing-range/index.html), which plays sounds and shows which animals can hear them.

**Grades:** 4–8. Lesson 1 works from grade 4. Lessons 2 and 3 use one multiplication or division each and suit grades 5–8.

**Before you start:** [What is a wave?](../03-waves/01-what-is-a-wave.md)

**Careful with sound:** keep the volume low when you press Play. Very high and very low tones can be uncomfortable, and loud sound can damage hearing.

**Hands on:** stretch a rubber band over an open box and pluck it, then tighten it and pluck again. Clap near a big wall and time the echo with a friend.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone how a bat catches a moth in the dark, and why the bass is the part that comes through the wall, you have this unit at level 4.
