Look closely enough at anything and it's made of tiny particles that never stop moving. This unit shows how that one idea explains temperature, heat, melting, boiling, and two of water's strangest tricks. One sim runs through it: [solid, liquid, gas](../../../sims/states-of-matter/index.html), where you heat and cool a cup of particles and watch them change state.

**Grades:** 4–8. All three lessons work from grade 4, with the experiments doing a lot of the explaining.

**Hands on:** food colouring dropped into a glass of hot water and a glass of cold water, side by side. An ice cube on a metal tray and one on a plastic plate: which melts first, and why?

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone why a metal bench feels colder than a wooden one, and why ice floats, you have this unit at level 4.
