Some of my favourite sessions are about things nobody can see. A magnet pushes without touching. Your phone talks to a router across the house, through a wall. A TV remote shines a light that your eyes can't detect, but a phone camera can. A bat finds a moth in the dark with a sound you'll never hear. This track makes those invisible things visible, with simulations and with simple experiments you can do at home.

**Try this at home:** point a TV remote at your phone's camera and press a button. You may see a flicker of light that your eyes alone can't see. That's infrared, and lesson 3 of [The light we can't see](./04-light-we-cant-see/03-infrared.md) explains it.
