| # | Lesson | The short version | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test yourself, don't reread | Trying to recall something strengthens memory far more than reading it again | Planned |
| 2 | Space it out | Short reviews over days beat one long session the night before | Planned |
| 3 | Mix your practice | Shuffle problem types so you learn to choose the method, not just apply it | Planned |
| 4 | Worked examples first | Study a solved problem, then a half-solved one, then solve one alone | Planned |
| 5 | Getting unstuck | Draw it, name the knowns and unknowns, try a simpler version | Planned |
| 6 | Exam strategy | Reading the question, budgeting time, checking units and signs | Planned |

Every lesson in this site already uses these ideas: **Check yourself** is retrieval practice, and **Your turn** is a faded worked example.
