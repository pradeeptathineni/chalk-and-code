Kinematics is the language of motion: where something is, how fast it is going, and how that is changing. No forces yet. That comes in Unit 2.

| 3 | [Speeding up: acceleration and the kinematic equations](./03-speeding-up.md) | 2 | motion graphs |
| 4 | [Projectile motion: two motions at once](./04-projectile-motion.md) | 2 | projectile motion |

**Before you start:** be comfortable with negative numbers and with rearranging a simple equation. Lessons 4 and 5 use sine and cosine to split a vector into parts.

**Unit check:** if you can explain to someone else why a ball at the top of its flight still has an acceleration, and why aiming straight across a river doesn't land you straight across, you have this unit at level 4.
