| # | Unit | What it covers | Interactive | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Functions and graphs | what a function is, transformations, reading graphs | slider-driven function plotter | Planned |
| 2 | Trigonometry | the unit circle, sine and cosine as motion, identities | point going round a circle tracing a sine wave | Planned |
| 3 | Vectors | components, adding vectors, dot product | drag-the-arrows vector adder | Planned |
| 4 | Calculus: rates of change | the derivative as slope, from secant to tangent | zoom-in tangent line | Planned |
| 5 | Calculus: accumulation | the integral as area, Riemann sums, the fundamental theorem | rectangles that shrink to the area | Planned |
| 6 | Probability and statistics | chance, distributions, sampling, the bell curve | coin flips and dice rolled thousands of times | Planned |

The vectors and calculus units link straight into [Physics: Motion](../physics/01-motion/index.md), where velocity is a slope and displacement is an area.
