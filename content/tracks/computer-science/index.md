| # | Unit | What it covers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Programming with p5.js | drawing, variables, `draw()` as a loop, input, then a bouncing ball with real gravity | Planned |
| 2 | Building a simulation | position, velocity and acceleration as three lines of code; making your own version of the [motion graphs](../../sims/motion-graphs/index.html) sim | Planned |
| 3 | Algorithms you can watch | searching, sorting, recursion, drawn step by step | Planned |
| 4 | Data structures | arrays, lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs | Planned |
| 5 | CS without a computer | binary, error correction, sorting networks, done with cards and paper | Planned |

Unit 2 is where computer science and physics meet: the update rule `v += a·dt; x += v·dt` is the same idea as the kinematic equations, done one small step at a time.
