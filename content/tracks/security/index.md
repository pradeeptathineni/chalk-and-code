These are the security lessons and workshops I teach, written for learners rather than for IT departments. For checklists, readiness levels and organisation-wide guidance, the companion [Ready, Secure, Go](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go) resource from neon1 goes deeper.

| # | Unit | What it covers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How attacks actually work | phishing, password guessing and reuse, scams, social engineering | Planned |
| 2 | Accounts and passwords | password managers, passkeys, two-factor sign-in | Planned |
| 3 | Devices and updates | why updates matter, backups, screen locks, lost devices | Planned |
| 4 | Privacy | what apps collect, settings that matter, sharing with care | Planned |
| 5 | Security for families and classrooms | talking about it with kids, school accounts, a household plan | Planned |
| 6 | How security works underneath | encryption with a padlock analogy, then the real idea; hashing; certificates | Planned |
